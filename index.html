Subscription Finder

Find the subscriptions and regular bills in your bank statements. Your files never leave your device.

Your data stays on your device

Your files are read and analyzed by this page, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, no analytics and no cookies, and the page loads nothing from other websites.

The page's security policy (Content-Security-Policy) blocks all network requests, so it could not send your data anywhere even if it tried. Once the page has loaded you can disconnect from the internet and it keeps working.

Your checklist is only saved if you turn on "Remember my checklist on this device".

How to check this yourself

  • Open your browser's developer tools (F12), go to the Network tab, then add a file: no new requests appear.
  • View the page source and find the Content-Security-Policy tag with connect-src 'none'.
  • The source code is public and short enough to read.

What this cannot see

  • Subscriptions billed through the App Store, Google Play or PayPal often appear as one combined charge, or not at all on your bank statement. Check those accounts separately.
  • Annual subscriptions need more than a year of transactions to show up. Most other patterns need at least three charges.
  • Results are suggestions based on dates and amounts. Check them before you cancel anything.
